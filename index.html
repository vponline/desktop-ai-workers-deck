Desktop AI Workers for Danish Businesses

Consult first. Deploy single-purpose automation assistants. Earn on setup and recurring improvement — not a mega-bot.

Sales-led in Denmark Build-led setup & tooling One job per worker
Why this conversation

The moment

  • Desktop AI agents are almost unused in Denmark — a real head start.
  • Live proof already exists: LinkedIn sales workers that find leads, engage, message, and book calls.
  • “Gotta make money on this — now is the time.”
  • Models keep improving; setup burden falls as assistants get more autonomous.
Market

The problem

Manual work still wins by default

Most Danish SMEs still do prospecting, outreach, email, and ops by hand.

AI is blocked at work

Many employers lock consumer AI on work PCs — leaving only weak internal tools.

Pressure without headcount

Job cuts and lean teams create urgency for leverage without hiring another FTE.

Token burn is real

Power users hit weekly limits in days without caps, rules, and efficiency tuning.

Solution

Consult → custom workers → recurring care

  • Enter as consultants — understand the business before installing anything.
  • Deploy custom, single-purpose desktop AI workers — not one mega-bot.
  • Client keeps the worker; we sell setup + ongoing updates and token tuning.
  • Later: free marketplace templates, with paid services on top.
Delivery

How it works

  1. Discovery consult — map workflows, tools, and constraints.
  2. Design one job per worker (sales, email, account management, market intel).
  3. Install & integrate — desktop agent, rules/workflows, efficiency review.
  4. Cap and tune token usage so the worker stays predictable.
  5. Ongoing MRR — model upgrades, prompt/script updates; later meta-workers for maintenance.

ICP

Who it’s for

  • Small Danish companies (e.g. construction and similar SMEs).
  • Owners/managers who feel sales & admin load but won’t hire another person yet.
  • Teams blocked from useful AI at work — need private desktop workers that fit their stack.
  • Not an employer pitch while a partner is on trial / fighting to keep a job.
Timing

Why now

  • Almost nobody in Denmark is doing this yet.
  • LinkedIn sales automation already feels production-ready for outreach.
  • Model trajectory points to more autonomous 24/7 workers soon.
  • Window: sell before the market catches up.

Partnership shape

Economics

Business model

Now

Upfront consultation / setup fee + MRR for updates, new-model adjustments, and token-usage improvements.

Value framing

Debated in chat: ~50% of a person → ~10% → landed around ~20% of a person, with higher leverage than a junior hire. No invented absolute prices.

Token policy

Reject open pay-per-use. Cap about $200–300 / week; overage → pay extra or wait for weekly reset. Teach efficiency so caps hold.

Later

Free public / marketplace bots → monetize deeper integration. Meta-workers maintain client workers to scale the hard MRR work.

GTM

Go-to-market

  • Sales-led by the Denmark partner; build-led by the technical partner.
  • First beachhead: small Danish companies with clear, repeatable workflows.
  • Story is easy to sell; maintenance is the hard part — and where recurring revenue lives.
  • After a couple of clients, reuse the same prompts/scripts for sales + secretary-style workers.

Reality check

Risks

  • Maintenance load vs. the MRR promise.
  • Client token usage discipline and overage friction.
  • No native paid bot marketplace → services model is required.
  • Employer AI bans / compliance if clients are regulated.
  • Don’t pitch a partner’s current employer while trial / job security is fragile.

  4. Draft a simple agreement: setup + MRR + token cap / overage.
  5. Stay conceptual until the first design partner closes.
Close
